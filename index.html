Capabilities

Built for the size of order that breaks a normal marketplace.

Every part of the flow assumes tonnes, not units — from who you're allowed to trade with down to how the money is held.

01

Counterparties are checked

Registration, trade history and bank verification cleared before a company can post or bid. Age of verification shows on every lot.

02

Lots, not line items

Post partial loads, full containers or standing monthly volume. Incoterms, packing and lead time sit on the lot itself.

03

Cross-border by default

Duty, documentation and freight quoted against the destination as you browse, so the landed number is the number you compare.

04

Prices you can audit

Settled trades publish anonymised clearing prices by grade and region. You price against the market, not against a rumour.

05

Matched on real constraints

Volume floor, grade, certification and shipping window are matching inputs — not filters you apply after the fact.

06

Funds held until release

Payment sits in escrow against inspection and documents. Released on agreed milestones, refunded if the load fails them.
